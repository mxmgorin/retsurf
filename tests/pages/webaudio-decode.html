decodeAudioData

Builds a 16-bit PCM wav in JavaScript at 48 kHz, decodes it with decodeAudioData, and loops it through an AudioBufferSourceNode. The context runs at its own rate, so the decoder has to resample: the tone must still come out at 441 Hz, and duration must stay 1.000 s.
